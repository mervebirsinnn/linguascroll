## V1 · put on / put up with (B1) · lifestyle
1. It was cold, so I put on my jacket and went outside.
2. My neighbor plays loud music every night, and I can't put up with it anymore.
3. I put on a little weight this winter, to be honest.
4. How much noise can you put up with?

Not (TR): **put on** = giymek (ayrıca kilo almak). **put up with** = katlanmak, tahammül etmek (bir şeye).

**Quiz V1-a** — "I can't ____ the noise anymore."
A) put on  B) put up with ✓  C) put off  D) put down
(put off = ertelemek; put down = bırakmak/aşağılamak; put on = giymek)

**Quiz V1-b** — "It's freezing! ____ your coat."
A) Put up with  B) Put off  C) Put on ✓  D) Put away
(put away = kaldırmak)

## V2 · get along with / keep up with (B1) · career
1. I get along with my coworkers very well.
2. My new boss is strict, but I get along with her too.
3. The course is fast, so it's hard to keep up with the lesson.
4. I read the news every day to keep up with what's happening.

Not (TR): **get along with** = iyi anlaşmak. **keep up with** = (hıza, gelişmelere) ayak uydurmak, geride kalmamak.

**Quiz V2-a** — "She is friendly, so she ____ everyone."
A) keeps up with  B) gets along with ✓  C) puts up with  D) takes after

**Quiz V2-b** — "The class is too fast. I can't ____ it."
A) get along with  B) keep up with ✓  C) put on  D) break through

## V3 · keep on / break through (B2) · career
1. Failure is part of learning, so keep on trying.
2. She kept on practicing even when nobody believed in her.
3. After months of work, the team finally broke through.
4. A breakthrough often comes right after you want to quit.

Not (TR): **keep on + V-ing** = (vazgeçmeden) devam etmek. **break through** = engeli aşmak, atılım yapmak; isim hâli **breakthrough** = büyük ilerleme.

**Quiz V3-a** — "He ____ talking, even though nobody was listening."
A) kept on ✓  B) kept up  C) put on  D) broke up
(kept on talking = konuşmayı sürdürdü; "kept up" + V-ing hatalıdır)

**Quiz V3-b** — "After years of research, the scientists made a major ____ in treating the disease."
A) breakdown  B) breakthrough ✓  C) break-in  D) breakout

## V4 · make up for / make up (B2) · lifestyle
1. I forgot your birthday, so I'll make up for it with a nice dinner.
2. You can't make up for lost time, but you can use today well.
3. They had a big fight, but they made up the next day.
4. Don't make up stories; just tell the truth.

Not (TR): **make up for** = telafi etmek. **make up** (kişiler) = barışmak. **make up** (hikâye) = uydurmak.

**Quiz V4-a** — "I was late, so I'll buy you lunch to ____ it."
A) make up for ✓  B) make up with  C) make out  D) make over
(make up with someone = biriyle barışmak)

**Quiz V4-b** — "They fought yesterday, but today they ____."
A) made up ✓  B) made out  C) made over  D) made of

## V5 · make / be made to / made of vs made from (B2) · humor
1. The movie made me cry, and my friend laughed at me.
2. As a child, I was made to clean my room every Saturday.
3. This chair is made of wood, but this paper is made from old trees.
4. My teacher made us repeat the sentence three times.

Not (TR): **make + kişi + fiil (to'suz)** = zorlamak/neden olmak: made me cry. Edilgende **to** gelir: I was made **to** clean. **made of** = hâlâ görülebilen malzeme; **made from** = dönüşmüş malzeme (kâğıt ağaçtan).

**Quiz V5-a** — "The teacher ____ us stay after class."
A) made  B) made to  C) was made  D) made for
Doğru: A (made us stay). *Not:* aynı soru edilgen yazılırsa "We ____ stay after class" → "were made to" (to şart).

**Quiz V5-b** — "He ____ to apologize to his sister."
A) made  B) was made ✓  C) was made for  D) made up
(He was made to apologize; "made" tek başına nesne ister)

**Quiz V5-c** — "This bridge is ____ steel."
A) made from  B) made by  C) made of ✓  D) made up

## Neden bu yapı
- Phrasal verb'ler tek başına çevrilemediği için en çok burada yanlış seçim yapılır; şıklar bilerek yakın (put on / put off / put up with).
- "make" ailesi (make sb do / be made to / make up / made of–from) sınavlarda hep karışan yer; bu yüzden tek videoda karşılaştırmalı.
- Her videonun 2 sorusu, o videonun içindeki cümleyle aynı bağlamdan gelir; ama çözmek için videoya özgü bilgi gerekmez.
