# Kalıp bankası (videolara serpiştirilecek ince ayrımlar) — TASLAK

Kendi yazdığım örnekler. DB/koda dokunulmadı. Her kalıp: örnek cümleler, Türkçe fark notu, yakın şıklı quiz. B1/B2 etiketi önerisidir.

---

## although / though / even though · despite / in spite of (B1-B2)

Örnek cümleler (video içinde doğal geçecek şekilde):
1. Although it was raining, we went for a walk.
2. We went for a walk despite the rain.
3. Despite being tired, she finished the report.
4. In spite of the traffic, he arrived on time.
5. He kept smiling, even though he was nervous.

Not (TR): **although / though / even though + özne + fiil** (tam cümle). **despite / in spite of + isim ya da V-ing**. "Despite of" diye bir kalıp YOK. Çok kullanılan hata: "Despite it was raining". Doğrusu: "Despite the rain" ya da "Despite the fact that it was raining". "Even though" = although'dan güçlü, şaşırtıcılığı vurgular.

Quiz AD-a — "____ it was cold, he went swimming."
A) Despite  B) In spite of  C) Although ✓  D) Despite of

Quiz AD-b — "She passed the exam ____ her illness."
A) although  B) even though  C) despite ✓  D) however

Quiz AD-c — "He went to work ____ feeling sick."
A) although  B) despite ✓  C) even though  D) in spite

## would rather (B1-B2)

Örnek cümleler:
1. I would rather stay at home tonight.
2. I'd rather drink tea than coffee.
3. Would you rather walk or take the bus?
4. I'd rather you called me tomorrow.
5. I'd rather not talk about it.
6. I'd rather have gone to the party yesterday.

Not (TR): **would rather + fiil (to'suz)** = tercih. **than** ile karşılaştırma. Olumsuz: **would rather not**. Başkasının eylemi için **would rather + özne + geçmiş zaman** (I'd rather you called…) = şimdiki/gelecek için bile geçmiş biçim. Geçmiş pişmanlık: **would rather have + V3**. Yaygın hata: "would rather to go".

Quiz WR-a — "I'd rather ____ at home tonight."
A) to stay  B) stay ✓  C) staying  D) stayed

Quiz WR-b — "I'd rather you ____ me tomorrow, not tonight."
A) call  B) called ✓  C) to call  D) calling

Quiz WR-c — "She'd rather walk ____ take the bus."
A) to  B) than ✓  C) from  D) as

## Önceki listeden devam eden kalıplar (örnek soru gelecek turda)
Phrasal verbs (put on/off/up with, get along with, keep up with/on, break through, make up for, look forward to, give up, run out of, turn down, bring up, carry on, come across, take after, figure out); fiil + edat (depend on, look after, good at, interested in); karışan fiil çiftleri (make/do, say/tell, bring/take, borrow/lend, lose/miss, rise/raise); be used to / used to / get used to; had better; it's time + past; so/such; too/enough; collocation (make a decision, pay attention, take a chance).

## Hangi videoya girer (öneri)
- although/despite: B1-3 (travel, hava-seyahat bağlamı) ya da yeni bir B2 videosu; "Although the flight was late, we enjoyed the trip. Despite the delay..."
- would rather: yeni B2 videosu (ör. tercih konuşması: "I'd rather… than…", "I'd rather you…").
- Her videoda en fazla 2 yeni kalıp; aynı kalıp sonraki videolarda tekrar geçer.
