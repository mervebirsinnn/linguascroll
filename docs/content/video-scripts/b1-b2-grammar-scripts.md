# B1 / B2 video senaryoları (gramer hedefli) — TASLAK

Kendi yazdığım özgün senaryolar. Her video 25-40 sn konuşma (≈70-100 sözcük), tek konuşmacı, düz kurgu. Satırlar tek tek altyazı segmentidir (kısa tutuldu; STT hattı gerçek zamanlamayı çıkaracak). Her videoda **hedef yapı** kalın işaretli ve altında Türkçe öğrenme notu var. DB'ye/koda dokunulmadı; MoneyPrinterTurbo (ya da başka araç) çıktısı sonradan bu metinle üretilir.

Senaryolar hedef yapıyı tekrarlar ama doğal konuşma gibi kalsın diye hikâyeye bağlanmıştır. Her videoda yalnız 1-2 hedef yapı var.

---

## B1-1 · used to (geçmiş alışkanlıklar) · topic: lifestyle
1. When I was a kid, I used to hate vegetables.
2. I used to push them to the side of my plate.
3. Now I eat salad every day, and I actually enjoy it.
4. My mom used to say, "One day you will thank me."
5. She was right, of course.
6. What did you use to hate when you were younger?

Not (TR): **used to + fiil** = geçmişte düzenli yapılan ama artık yapılmayan alışkanlık. Soru/olumsuzda "use to" olur: "Did you use to…?"

## B1-2 · Present Perfect + for / since · topic: lifestyle
1. I have lived in this city for five years.
2. I have worked at the same café since 2021.
3. I have known my best friend for ten years.
4. She has lived here since she was a child.
5. How long have you lived in your city?

Not (TR): **have/has + V3** + **for** (süre: for five years) / **since** (başlangıç noktası: since 2021). Şu anda hâlâ süren durumlar için.

## B1-3 · First Conditional + when/until · topic: travel
1. If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at the hotel.
2. If the weather is nice, we will go to the beach.
3. We will not leave until the bus arrives.
4. When we get to the city, I will call you.
5. If you miss the train, you will have to wait an hour.

Not (TR): **If + Simple Present, will + fiil** = gerçekçi gelecek ihtimali. If/when/until cümlesinde "will" kullanılmaz.

## B1-4 · must / have to / don't have to / should · topic: career
1. You must wear a helmet on a bike.
2. In my job, I have to start at eight o'clock.
3. On Saturdays I don't have to work, so I sleep late.
4. You should drink more water, honestly.
5. You shouldn't check your phone all night.

Not (TR): **must/have to** = zorunluluk; **don't have to** = zorunlu DEĞİL (yasak değil); **should** = tavsiye.

---

## B2-1 · Third Conditional + wish (pişmanlık) · topic: career
1. If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam.
2. I wish I had started earlier.
3. If I had asked for help, things might have been different.
4. I should have listened to my teacher.
5. But it's fine, because I learned something important.

Not (TR): **If + Past Perfect, would have + V3** = geçmişte olmamış durumun hayali sonucu. **I wish + Past Perfect** = geçmiş pişmanlığı. **should have + V3** = yapmalıydım.

## B2-2 · must have / might have / can't have (geçmişe çıkarım) · topic: humor
1. The lights were off, so he must have gone to bed.
2. She isn't answering, so she might have lost her phone.
3. He can't have forgotten our meeting, he put it in his calendar.
4. They could have taken a different road and gotten lost.
5. So many possibilities, and not one answer!

Not (TR): **must have + V3** = neredeyse kesin çıkarım; **might/could have** = olasılık; **can't have** = olamaz (kesin değil ama çok güçlü olumsuz çıkarım).

## B2-3 · Causative (have/get something done) + passive · topic: lifestyle
1. Last week I had my car repaired, and it's still not perfect.
2. I'm getting my hair cut tomorrow.
3. The new bridge was built in only two years.
4. The report is being checked at the moment.
5. Have you ever had your photo taken by a professional?

Not (TR): **have/get + nesne + V3** = bir işi başkasına yaptırmak. **Passive** = yapılan işe odaklanma (was built, is being checked).

## B2-4 · Reported speech (soru/emir/ifade) · topic: career
1. My manager said that the meeting had been moved to Friday.
2. She asked me whether I could take notes.
3. He told me to send the report by noon.
4. They asked where I had worked before.
5. I said that I would think about it.

Not (TR): Dolaylı anlatımda zaman bir kademe geriler (will→would, Present Perfect→Past Perfect). Soru: **asked + if/whether/wh-** ve düz cümle sırası (where I had worked). Emir: **told + kişi + to + fiil**.

## Üretim notları
- Metni değiştirmeden okutmak yeterli; STT hattı gerçek ses üzerinden zaman damgalarını ve segmentleri çıkarır (kısa satırlar zaten doğal duraklar).
- Her satır için İngilizce/Türkçe açıklama (altyazı penceresi) STT sonrası, her segmentin gerçek metnine göre yazılır; yukarıdaki "Not (TR)" kısımları o açıklamaların çekirdeğidir.
- Seviye etiketi: B1-1..4 → B1, B2-1..4 → B2 (gramer hedefine göre; kelime düzeyi bilerek sade).
- Kapsam dışı kalanlar (sonraki tur): B1 Past Continuous / Passive basic, B2 Present Perfect Continuous / Past Perfect Continuous, participle clauses, defining/non-defining relative clauses (mevcut B2 videolarında zaten var).
