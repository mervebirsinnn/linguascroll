## A1

**a1-ielts-ask-for-directions** — Havalimanı, turist ve görevli.
- Tourist: Excuse me, I am lost. Where is the **baggage claim**?
- Staff: Go straight, then turn left. It is next to the café.
- Tourist: Thank you! Is it far?
- Staff: No, it is only two minutes.

**a1-ielts-black-coffee** — Kafe, sipariş.
- Barista: Good morning! What would you like?
- Customer: One **black coffee**, please. No milk, no sugar.
- Barista: Anything else?
- Customer: Yes, a small cake. It is my **big day** today!

**a1-ielts-apply-for-a-job** — İki arkadaş.
- Ali: I want to **apply for a job** at the bakery.
- Zeynep: Great idea! Are you **afraid of** the interview?
- Ali: A little. I am not good at talking.
- Zeynep: Don't worry. I will help you. **Best wishes**!

**a1-ielts-after-dark** — Parkta anne ve çocuk.
- Mum: It is late. It is **after dark** now.
- Boy: Can I play five more minutes?
- Mum: No, it is **against the rules** to stay out. We go home.
- Boy: Okay. I'm afraid not... I mean, okay!

**a1-ielts-all-the-time** — Komşular.
- Anna: Do you walk your dog every day?
- Tom: Yes, **all the time**! He loves the park.
- Anna: My cat sleeps **all the time**.
- Tom: Haha! Maybe it is **about time** for her to play.

## C1

**c1-ielts-the-big-picture** — Toplantı.
- Manager: We keep arguing about small details. Let's look at **the big picture**.
- Analyst: Agreed. Our **best practices** are outdated, and we're falling **below average** in retention.
- Manager: Then we overhaul the onboarding process rather than patching it.
- Analyst: That would address the root cause, not just the symptoms.

**c1-ielts-black-sheep** — Aile yemeği.
- Elif: Everyone treats me like the **black sheep** just because I left law school.
- Dad: That's not how we see it. We were simply **taken aback**.
- Elif: Following my passion isn't a **betrayal**; it's a calculated risk.
- Dad: Then show us the plan, and we'll hear you out.

**c1-ielts-black-ice** — Sabah yolu, iş arkadaşları.
- Liam: I nearly skidded off the road. **Black ice** is practically invisible.
- Sara: You should have taken the **back roads** instead of the motorway.
- Liam: They're no safer when it freezes overnight.
- Sara: True. Next time, let's carpool until the thaw.

**c1-ielts-remote-work-debate** — Podcast tartışması.
- Host: Is remote work **a double-edged sword**?
- Guest: Undoubtedly. It boosts autonomy, yet it **blurs the boundary** between work and rest.
- Host: So the benefits come at a hidden cost?
- Guest: Precisely, and companies rarely **account for** that burnout.

**c1-ielts-alternative-medicine** — Doktor ve hasta.
- Patient: Would **alternative medicine** complement my treatment?
- Doctor: Some practices **alleviate** symptoms, but evidence varies considerably.
- Patient: So it's not a substitute?
- Doctor: Correct. Consider it **supplementary**, never a replacement.

## C2

**c2-ielts-nuance-of-persuasion** — Akademik söyleşi.
- Prof: Rhetoric rarely **hinges on** facts alone; it **exploits** what the audience is already predisposed to believe.
- Student: So persuasion is less about proof than **resonance**?
- Prof: Quite. The most **insidious** arguments feel like our own conclusions.

**c2-ielts-unintended-consequences** — Politika yorumcusu.
- Pundit: Well-intentioned regulation can **backfire** spectacularly, **engendering** the very harm it sought to **curtail**.
- Host: Can you give an example?
- Pundit: Rent controls: **ostensibly** protective, yet they often **stifle** supply and **exacerbate** scarcity.

**c2-ielts-language-and-thought** — Dilbilimci.
- Linguist: The claim that language **circumscribes** thought is **tenuous** at best.
- Host: Yet vocabulary does shape what we **readily** notice.
- Linguist: Granted, but **shaping attention** is hardly **dictating cognition**; the distinction is **non-trivial**.

**c2-ielts-art-and-restraint** — Küratör, galeride.
- Curator: What **unsettles** viewers here isn't excess, but **restraint**.
- Visitor: The emptiness feels almost **deliberate**.
- Curator: It is. The **void** does the **heavy lifting**; the artist **withholds** so you **fill in** the rest.

**c2-ielts-moral-ambiguity** — İki arkadaş, kitap.
- Maya: What makes the protagonist **compelling** is that she's **irreconcilably** flawed.
- Jon: A **morally ambiguous** lead forces readers to **grapple with** their own **complicity**.
- Maya: Exactly. The novel **eschews** easy verdicts, and it's **richer** for it.
